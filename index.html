Chapter I — Four Decades of Legacy

The Sangitam 1983 Story

From a single bus en route between Jalgaon and Pune to over 100+ company-owned luxury AC sleeper coaches across Maharashtra.

1983 — First Journey

Founded by Mr. Vinod Patil

Sangitam Travels was established with a single bus operating between Jalgaon and Pune. Built on personal commitment, punctuality, and passenger safety.

2005 — Fleet Expansion

Connecting Maharashtra Hubs

Expanded inter-city routes to Nashik, Sambhajinagar (Aurangabad), Bhusawal, Dhule, and Mumbai, establishing regional booking hubs.

2018 — Luxury Era

Induction of Mercedes-Benz & Volvo

Modernized the fleet under Director Dhanvijay Patil, introducing air-suspension sleeper berths and live GPS tracking for passenger safety.

2026 — Digital Leader

100+ Buses & Express Cargo

Spearheaded by Director Dharmasen Patil, offering seamless online seat booking, mobile app tracking, and express cargo parcel logistics across Maharashtra.
