Sangitam Travels Is Well known Company of India.

Established in 1983, Sangitam Travels began its journey with just one bus service operating between Jalgaon and Pune. Sangitam now proudly operates a fleet of over 100 company-owned buses, making Sangitam one of the largest private bus operators in Maharashtra. Covers more than 80% of the state, along with key routes connecting neighbouring state capital cities.

For over four decades, Sangitam Travels remained committed to serving the evolving needs of travellers across India, with an unwavering focus on Quality, Comfort, Safety, and Reliability.

Our Legacy

40 Years of Excellence in Road Transport

1983 • FOUNDING YEAR
The First Bus Journey

Founder Mr. Vinod Patil launched Sangitam Travels in Jalgaon with just a single bus operating between Jalgaon and Pune, filling a crucial rural connectivity gap.

1 Bus Service • Jalgaon ➔ Pune
View Era Details
What started as a modest music house ("Sangitam") soon grew into Maharashtra's lifeline for students, working professionals, and families seeking reliable intercity transport.
1998 • EXPANSION
Express Parcel Cargo Network

Introduced daily same-night parcel logistics using dedicated under-deck storage across Jalgaon, Dhule, Pune, and Mumbai hubs.

Same-Night Express Cargo
View Era Details
Sangitam Express Logistics became North Maharashtra's most trusted express parcel carrier, helping traders, businesses, and individuals ship goods with guaranteed next-morning arrival.
2012 • FLEET UPGRADE
