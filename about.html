Our 40+ Years Legacy

From a single bus operating between Jalgaon and Pune in 1983 to a leading private bus and parcel logistics operator in Maharashtra.

The Founder's Vision

Over Four Decades of Service

Founded in 1983 by visionary entrepreneur Mr. Vinod Patil, Sangitam Travels began its journey with a single bus operating between Jalgaon and Pune.

Over four decades of dedicated service, the company has expanded into one of Maharashtra's premier private bus operators. Today, Sangitam Travels operates a modern fleet of over 100 company-owned luxury AC sleeper coaches, connecting major cities, industrial hubs, and pilgrimage centers across Maharashtra and inter-state destinations.
